## More Aquatic Life Added to Lake Felicity

Lake Felicity’s supply of fish has been a bit lower than usual lately, so Zachary Blackwood ordered a few more slimy swimmers. Yes, you can do that! Any ambitious fishermen may now have increased chances at catching something for supper. 

## Celebrating at the Symphony

This week BBSG was able to attend the Symphony on the Prairie at Conner Prairie in Indianapolis, along with the Sturm and Hart families, for the 4th of July (although we went on the 2nd). This year the first few hours were remarkably hot, but thankfully some of the freebies were paddle fans and red white and blue popsicles, which many in our group enjoyed three or four of. After a few hours of ferociously waving fans and eating picnic dinner, the music began and the sun set, so the rest of the evening was not quite so stifling. The musical show ended with a colorful array of fireworks exploding to the right of the stage, combining the tune of the orchestra with the loud booming. Around midnight, we returned home and collapsed into bed, exhausted from the fun and exciting night. 

## BBSG Sees a 60% Drop in Enrollment This Year

Statistics are not looking good for enrollment at Blackwood Boarding School this coming fall, as the majority of students are dropping out! In fact, so far the grand total number of students for 2026-27 is only…two! “I’m feeling excited because there will be nobody to bother me anymore. But I think I’ll be a little bored.” said half of the school (Allison Blackwood). “I feel excited too!” stated the other 50% (Lottie Blackwood). Before now, BBSG was already expecting to lose 20% of students to Harrison Highschool, but where are the others headed off to? Former Blackwood Boarding Girl Elsie B. explains: “Me and Emory might be going to Lafayette Chritian this year. Well, we’ve sent in our application thingies, but we might not be accepted. But we are assuming we’ll get in. I think it will be interesting to go to a christian school instead of homeschooling.” Thankfully all three schools (LCS, HHS, and BBSG) share the same vacation schedule, so everyone residing at the Manor will have the same school breaks. But, this coming year will definitely be a big adjustment for everyone, especially the Blackwood Boarding head teacher, who we’re hoping won’t get too emotional.
