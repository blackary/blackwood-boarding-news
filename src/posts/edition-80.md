## More than Corn in Indiana

All Blackwood Manor residents along with the Hart family were able to enjoy our final bit of summer this week with one last delightful outing, and Indiana Beach was the perfect place to do that! “It was really fun!” said student Allie B., “My favorite thing was the Cornball ride.” And Principal Z stated, “I thought that it was quite a nice experience…The rides were fun, the lines were short, but the water was a little expensive.” It was convenient that the park was big enough that there was always something to do, but small enough that the older children could go places by themselves, while the seven-and-under-year-olds mainly enjoyed splashing in the lazy river or riding the Mini Motors and others in Kiddieland. Many (including teacher Flo Blackwood!) enjoyed the huge loop-de-loop ride, as well as the famous Hoosier Hurricane! We ended the day with the Faris aunts’ favorite ride, the swings, and a lovely ice cream shop on the way home. 

## From BBSG to HHS 

Former Blackwood Boarding Girl Felicity Blackwood moved on from our small academy this week, and is now officially a student of Harrison High School. We had an A+ interview with her, probably the most cooperative we’ve ever had, as a matter of fact. When asked how her first day went, Felicity responded, “Well, at first I was getting lost after every other class, but I eventually figured out how to get to most of my classes. Though I have yet to make it from biology to math without getting lost, even while using my color-coded map.” Felicity also informed us that (so far) her favorite subjects are 2D art and English. “The assignments haven’t been too hard yet.” says Felicity, “And most of the teachers give us time in class to at least get started on our homework, which is nice.” Well, since the first few days have seemed to have gone smoothly enough, it looks like last year's BBSG graduating class will be highly successful at HHS.
