## Fresh Eggs 

This week the Blackwood Manor hens have finally started laying! Allison and Lottie B. were the first to discover three small brown eggs in the coop, but instead of displaying them proudly as trophies in the BBSG hall of fame, the two girls scrambled them for lunch. The last one they saved for the rest of the students to split when they came home from their day schools. The chickens have now laid over a dozen little eggs so far, and we will hopefully soon start receiving a consistent eight eggs a day. Let’s just hope we can eat that many!

## Dance, Music, and Sports

“We just started ballet,” said Charlotte (Lottie) Blackwood after her and Allison’s first ballet practice, “and we REALLY like it, and I really like doing their moves, and I really like to do ballet, so yeah!” “We have to wear a purple leotard,” added Allie, “with convertible tights and cream-colored ballet shoes. Me and Lottie’s favorite thing that we did was we would go on our tiptoes with our hands on our waist and go in a circle…” But Allie and Lottie weren’t the only ones starting extra-carriculars this week. Emory Blackwood has begun learning the flute and Elsie Blackwood has started practices for her volleyball team (both at LCS)! It looks like all Blackwood Manor residents will have plenty of performances and games to go to this semester!

## More Wheels on the Bus Going ‘Round

Principal Zachary Blackwood and Headmistress Flo B. finally went school bus shopping on Saturday, since the only one we owned was the tiny, very cramped van we had towed around the country with very unreliable air conditioning. They were successful and found a much more spacious blue eight-passenger with working AC and even…an intercom!
