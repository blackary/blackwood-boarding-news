## BBSG’s Three-Day Weekend Celebrations

This week Blackwood Boarding, LCS, and Harrison, like most schools in the U.S., were given a glorious day off last Monday to celebrate Labor Day! Blackwood Manor was stress-tested as Principal Z likes to say, when over 40 family and friends came over to enjoy outdoor activities, BBQ food, and board games. “It was really fun except that me and Margarette \[a Faris cousin] couldn’t swim in the pond because she broke her arm \[prior to visiting the Manor], so instead we just played with the kittens and played some board games.” Even though so many people were able to attend, with the big yard, pond, and large indoor spaces the party didn’t feel too crowded at all. In fact, the event remained fairly non-chaotic throughout the day. It looks like Blackwood Manor has officially passed its Stress-Test!

## Breaking News: The Last First

This week, for the last time ever, one Blackwood Boarding Girl lost her first tooth! Student Lottie B. was very excited when she discovered the wiggly little chomper in the bottom front of her mouth. Her older and wiser sister Allison eagerly yanked the tooth out for her, leaving one bloody, slobbery mess. Lottie was thrilled to receive a sparkling golden dollar from the tooth fairy that night after placing the pearly white under her pillow. Her advice for any future tooth-loosers: “It doesn’t hurt at all, but it hurts more if your dad pulls it out than your sister. And it’s really fun to get a gold coin!” 

## Corgi Party for the Birthday Boy and Girl

September 12th, 1949 was the birthday of Grandpa Faris, which we celebrated on Saturday along with the birthday of Allison Blackwood who was born 68 years later on the same day! To celebrate the occasion, the birthday-buddies blew out nine candles on a delicious Dairy Queen ice cream cake at their corgi-themed birthday party. Happy Birthday Allison and G’pa!
