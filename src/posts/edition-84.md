## El Muro and The Knights 

You may have thought the days of the great legend El Muro were over (See edition #4 for more information.) but ‘The Wall’ is back (volleyball version)! Elsie had her first two games with the LCS Knights this week, and was very excited when they won their first game against Faith Christian’s team. They were sadly defeated by Klondike Middle a few days later, but to be fair the public school is much larger than LCS. When asked to elaborate on her winning game, the world-famous El Muro responded, “Be quiet.” 

## Chalk Chalk and more Chalk

This Saturday was the annual Sidewalk Chalk Contest held in BBSG’s old old neighborhood. This year’s grand prize winner was (the same as last year)…everyone! “I was very impressed with Elsie’s horse drawing.” said Flo Blackwood, “It was also fun to meet up with our old old neighbors. Not old like old old, but old as in previous.” All BBSG students were proudly presented with magnificent prizes, including pens, stickers, and writing utensil holders. It was a lovely event, as usual, and a fun friendly competition. 

## Murder Mystery at Blackwood Manor

Screams echoed through the dark house, the dead body of one of our own lay (somehow still talking) sprawled out on the ground, poked to death by an imposter in our midst. Another murder had occurred! All BBSG students, along with some Spirydovich cousins and Lutz friends gathered in the Blackwood Manor Library to discuss who the killer was this time in the exciting, but lighthearted, murder mystery game. Let Flo B. know if you would like to participate in the next thrilling game of Imposters at the Manor!
