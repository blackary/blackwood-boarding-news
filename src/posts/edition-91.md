## One Birthday and a Triple Birthday Party

This Thursday one former BBSG student celebrated her 15th birthday, and of course took the driver’s permit test that day. It was successful, and BBSG now has their newest permitted driver, Felicity Blackwood! Then on Friday, she, Elsie, and Quinn Karshen, who is actually not a BBSG student or Blackwood Manor resident, but was spending the night at the Manor on her real birthday, all celebrated their birthdays with a little party! Several families were able to come and enjoy some volleyball in the backyard, s’mores, an ice cream bar, and games! 

## A Lovely Back Porch now Open for Business

The long-awaited finishing of the Blackwood Manor back porch is finally reaching its completion, and was first test-runned at the triple birthday party of Felicity, Elsie, and Quinn. “My favorite part is just sitting on the swing…” said designer Flo B., who then added, “in silence.” Though not always peaceful and quiet, the porch is loved by all residents and used often by students, staff, and unfortunately cats. The local wildlife however, will hopefully be sealed outside once the screen door comes in within the week. 

## BBSG’s Multiplying Candy Stash

This past Saturday Blackwood Manor-ians dawned their creative costumes and drove (with their newest chauffeur) to the former neighborhood of BBSG’s residence. This year the displayed costumes were (left to right in the photo) a cook, a minion, an old fashioned western girl, a cat, an aldi bag, and a gumball machine. Between the six costumes and the bowl of candy already present at Blackwood Manor, we will be fully equipped with plentiful candy for the next few months at least. Feel free to stop by and ask for a piece (or 5 or 6)!
