## Goodbye to 2547 Nottingham 

“Well we cleaned it out, we did one last mowing job and then, (fake sob) we said goodbye!” said Flo B. after finally selling the former BBSG building. “Mom, you’re acting like they're gonna demolish it and build a parking lot.” Elsie Blackwood replied to Flo’s overly-dramatic sentimental comments. However, letting go of our former school building has been bittersweet, as we bid farewell to Nottingham Place and now settle in at Blackwood Manor.

## Bashing at the Hotel

This week was the highly successful Blackwood Bash of 2026, held at the local Drury Inn. The pool room was exceptionally enjoyed by the bashers, especially the younger ones who would swim for hours whenever possible. The free buffet dinners and breakfasts are also highly recommended by the extended Blackwood family. One of the bashers, Rosie Mann, would like to mention that she invented a fun new game where you “pretend to be a princess and your mom dies and you go to a mean orphanage with a nice duchess and a mean duchess.” Another game that the children enjoyed was a multiple teams around-the-hotel picture scavenger hunt, with photo challenges like “your whole team doing YMCA in the front of the building” and “making a funny face with the front desk clerk”. The hotel party was a lovely time that will be fondly remembered by all attendees.
