## Mysterious Robbery Under Investigation

Earlier this week, BBSG was shockingly broken into, revealing some major security deficiencies at the school, and creating a perplexing mystery! Thankfully only one valuable item appears to have been stolen. The robber(s) were so quick that no witnesses saw it happening. Authorities have started to look into the situation, but the crime scene was carefully swept clean of any evidence. All Blackwood Manor residents claim to have had nothing to do with the stolen stapler, but some suspect that there may be an inside man in this seemingly perfect and seamless operation. Reporters will continue to investigate, but it seems that most authorities now believe that this important piece of office equipment will simply be found somewhere it was "misplaced" around the house. The public however, is not so sure this was merely an accident. 

## Egg Laying Full Speed Ahead

All eight hens at Blackwood Manor are now regularly laying large eggs every day. In fact it’s getting hard for students to keep up! Oftentimes more eggs are found than chickens. Though this is likely because the coop is not always checked every night. The entire school loves (for now) going out to check on the chickens and collect as many eggs as possible, sometimes needing to take multiple trips. If anyone out there has been looking for some fresh eggs, head over to BBSG for a steal price plus the friends and family discount!

## A Successful Tournament for El Muro

The volleyball champion El Muro faced her biggest challenge yet this week at the Indiana Christian school volleyball tournament! The LCS Knights won third place overall, but El Muro managed to score the team’s winning point in their second game. The team cheered loudly for themselves chanting “S-C-E-A Ooooh S!” Or something like that anyway, it was hard to comprehend in the loud gym.
