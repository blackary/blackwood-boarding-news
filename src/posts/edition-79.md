## BBSG At the Dunes

After three weeks of rescheduling due to intense winds, high waves, and thunderstorms, BBSG along with some Sturm and Faris cousins were finally able to take our day trip up to Indiana Dunes State Park (aka–the beach). “After such a long wait we had beautiful weather and we’re thankful no one died.” stated Flo Blackwood. Despite her and Aunt Anna’s worries, no children were swept away by riptides or attacked by very lost sharks. “The state park people wouldn’t let us boogie board,” explained one Blackwood Boarding student, “but then we realized we could just walk across the border into the national park where it was legal.” After this realization a few from our cousin group enjoyed riding the waves without the supervision of restrictive lifeguards. When not splashing in the water, our group enjoyed climbing the dunes, building sand castles, and dining on a nutritious lunch of Cheez-Its, Oreos, lemonade, (some) fruit, and (a very small amount of) crudités. Overall it was a delightful day with cousins on the lovely beach. 

## Cousin Slumber Party 

Blackwood Boarding Girls’ cousin Liesl Sturm was able to stay at the Manor for a few days this week after the dunes adventure, and she and Emory B. had a splendid time sleepover-ing. The cousins enjoyed a day of shopping with Mom/Aunt Flo with ice cream at the end (mainly to use as bribery for everyone to keep calm). “It was really fun.” said Emory, and that’s all we could get out of her. 

## Blackwood Boarding Prima Ballerinas 

This coming school year, students Allison and Lottie Blackwood (otherwise known as the entire school) are ecstatic to be enrolled in a real ballerina class. The two aspiring dancers have already received their ballet shoes and leotards, and can’t wait to be twirling on stage.
