## Adventures with the Mann Cubs

Fall break arrived this week, and all of Blackwood Manor packed their bags and headed up to the frigid North Country of New York, to visit our Aunt, Uncle, and cousins. Unfortunately, Aunt Charity, Uncle Garrett, and the latest addition to their family, Moriah Mann, were not able to be at home when BBSG visited as they needed to stay at the hospital in a city a few hours away. However, BBSG was still able to have a very fun time with the cousins who weren’t in the hospital, and we were able to visit many exciting places during our week there. On Monday we visited the library, went with the cousins to their flu shots and ice cream to celebrate, and went to a historic home in a town nearby. Though, most cousins were more focused on the play room than the artifacts and reading about the history of the building. On Tuesday was a trip to a children’s science museum in Canada with an amazing liquid nitrogen show in which one member of our group got to volunteer! The next day everyone enjoyed visiting a fall activity farm, and having some delicious fresh cinnamon donuts! After returning home, a little play was performed which had actually been written and produced by the cousins that week titled, “A Girl Called Tallahassee”. Thursday morning was spent at Lampsend falls, a nearby waterfall park, and of course a trip to the ice cream shop afterwards. Finally, very early on Friday it was time for BBSG to head home. On our way back, we were able to stop in the city where Moriah’s hospital was located, and visit Aunt Charity, Uncle Garrett, and even Moriah herself! On the long drive home, everyone agreed that it had been a wonderful fall break spending time with long-lost New Yorkian cousins. 

## A Girl Called Tallahassee: This Year’s Must-See 

The Milk and Honey Theater Company outdid themselves this week with their latest and arguably greatest performance: A Girl Called Tallahassee! The show (starring Grace Mann) seamlessly encompasses drama, comedy, tearjerking experiences and heartwarming moments. The entertaining story and incredible acting are sure to make this play an instant classic, and a highly revered work of art. It will definitely be receiving a 5-star review, and is highly recommended as a must-see production for all ages.
