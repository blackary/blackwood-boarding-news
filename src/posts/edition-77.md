## Two Tragic Losses and Three New Arrivals

On Tuesday, July 14th 2026, a much beloved and adored kitten, KitCat, was fatally hit by a vehicle on the driveway of Angelic Lane. Her body was discovered that afternoon by Flo Blackwood on her afternoon walk. Allison B. had been searching for her missing cat all day and was heartbroken to hear that her new pet was dead. The funeral and burial were held the next day after Allison had adopted a new cat from the Faris Farm who she named Twix. But to add to the sadness, that very evening, Pumpkin, Emory Blackwood’s kitten, climbed under and into the car unnoticed and didn’t jump out until Flo Blackwood had driven miles away to downtown Lafayette. The scared little cat dashed into the woods and was never seen again. Emory was devastated. Neither of these incidents helped convince Principal Zachary to allow any more felines at BBSG, but he was eventually persuaded and the next day Emory adopted a replacement kittie from Great Grandma and Grandpa Blackwood’s. And Principal Z even let Felicity and Elsie B. take home one extra kitten (probably to make certain we wouldn’t need to go back). The newest arrivals were named Sophie Softpaws, and Wilbur (since he was the runt). Zachary Blackwood has now officially stated that BBSG will NOT be taking any more kittens home, but will have to keep these four alive and well. 

## Patio and Driveway Construction

This week some piles of rocks were put in our backyard and outside the garage. The gravel mounds are actually going to have a purpose, because cement will soon be poured over them and BBSG will have a patio and partial driveway. This development is apparently very exciting because Flo B. stated, “I cannot wait to have a rug outside the door to wipe off all the mud. It is going to be LIFECHANGING. And, we’re gonna be eating outside all the time!” 

## Fun at the Y

This week Blackwood Boarding had planned to take a field trip to the Indiana Dunes with Sturm cousins, but unfortunately the weather at the Dunes was predicted to be stormy. However, everyone was still able to have a splashing time since we were able to swim at the YMCA outdoor pool in Indianapolis where it was warm and sunny.
