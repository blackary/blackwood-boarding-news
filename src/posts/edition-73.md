## Space Probe Mission Complete

Last Friday came the end of this years’ Redeeming Grace Vacation Bible School. But the last two days were filled with fun and excitement from the missionary stories, to craft time, to the entertaining astronaut skit (in which everyone’s favorite character, Captain Cosmos welcomed the Space Probe astronauts to ‘Mission Control’.) The final day included the thrilling, and soaking, Water Day at recreation run by Zachary and Flo Blackwood, our very own principal and head teacher at BBSG! The space themed VBS definitely wore out the adult helpers, but it was a complete success that was greatly enjoyed by the young astronauts. 

## Latest Class Pets at BBSG

After many weeks of anticipation, students Lottie and Allison Blackwood were finally able to take home their 6-and-a-half week old kittens from the Farm. Both girls are loving their new furry pets, and have affectionately named them Firework and KitCat. “Firework is really cute and adorable,” said Lottie, “and she’s a cat, and she really likes sniffing things and she’s really cute and yeah.” While Allison stated, “Mine is named KitCat because it’s a cat and I really like KitKats.” While these girls enjoy taking care of their little felines, they will hopefully grow up to be excellent mice hunters, as numerous rodents and mouse droppings have been discovered around the house. 

## Lake Felicity Filled to the Brim

The unusually large amount of June rain this year has been filling up the pond on Blackwood Manor, also known as Lake Felicity. While it may not have been officially classified as a lake earlier, with the recent downpours the water has risen a very significant amount, and no BBSG students can even touch the bottom anymore. Students have enjoyed swimming and boating in the Lake for the past week, and it can now be confirmed that it currently serves as an excellent swimming hole.
