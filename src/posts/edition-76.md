## Happy Birthday to Lottie

Congratulations to student Lottie Blackwood, who turned six-years-old this past Tuesday! Blackwood Boarding celebrated by taking a cat cake (no felines were harmed in its’ creation.) and some ice cream to enjoy at the Faris farm. Lottie was very excited to receive her presents which she had been anticipating for nearly three months. This year she unpackaged two doll house babies, a mini drawing pad, a pink glittery stuffed animal, a book, and, the highlights, a porcelain tea set from Grandma Faris, and her very own snow cone machine! It was quite the birthday haul, even for Lottie, but the snow cones have been a delight to many students and visitors to BBSG. Happy Birthday Lottie and enjoy your year of being six!

## World Cup Watching

This week, as you may have known, was the FIFA World Cup soccer game between England and Norway! BBSG, well, most of BBSG, well actually only principal Zachary was proud to watch the entire game with the Wilburn family and our Indian friend Vivek, while everyone else ate food and chatted, occasionally peeking in to see who was winning. The hamburger sliders, nachos, root beer, and snow cones (courtesy of Lottie Blackwood’s new machine) were especially enjoyed by the less engaged soccer fans. Oh, and England won in case you were wondering!

## House Progress Continues

Work on Blackwood Manor continued since we moved in, but a few major projects were completed this week! The front door was finally installed and stained to a warmer, more welcoming shade, and the stone around the fireplace and outside of the chimney were put up. “It makes our house so much more cozy. Oh my word, I love it!” said Flo B. And Zachary Blackwood thought, “\[The stone fireplace] gives the room some manliness to contrast the pink chairs and flower pillows.” Now that the door, fireplace, and chimney are completed, the finishing touches on the interior of the house can be wrapped up.
