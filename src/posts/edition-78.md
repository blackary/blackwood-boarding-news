## Cemented in Place

This week the cement for our back patio and driveway was poured in! The event was very exciting for BBSG. Many students enjoyed watching as they poured the cement and smoothed it out. Girls and the school dog loved doing hand/paw prints in the fresh mud like celebrities, which can now be viewed by any visitors or tourists. Photos are free, but autographs will cost up to five dollars. Now that the driveway, front walkway, and patio are finished students can enjoy drawing with sidewalk chalk and scootering, while Flo Blackwood is loving the ability to now eat outdoors. 

## BBSG At the County Fair

The Tippecanoe County Fair was held this week in Lafayette, which Blackwood Boarding was able to attend a couple of times. With teacher Flo B. supervising, the school toured the 4-H projects, went through the animal barns, and collected freebies. Then later in the week when principal Z was able to join, students enjoyed some exciting rides. Teacher Flo B. attempted to participate, but found herself sick after her second thrilling jaunt. Still, everyone was able to have fun and would recommend the fair this year.

## A Final Farewell to Classy

The Blackwood Boarding former schoolbuilding (otherwise known as an RV or Repulsively Vitriolic) was driven down to Camping World to be sold back to them to go on to a better life. The entire family was able to ride in her one last time, and while we probably won’t exactly miss her, it was sentimental for some to say goodbye to our mobile home, who served us semi-faithfully for over nine long long long (long) months. Now there will be a final inspection to ensure there are no major issues and then Classy will officially belong to Camping World once more.
