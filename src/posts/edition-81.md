## From BBSG to LCS

This week, yet another educational transition as two more students left BBSG for another institution, Lafayette Christian School. “It was, in four words:” said Emory Blackwood, “easy, long, sleepy, and fun. I wish that I could keep homeschooling.” Emory also stated that there were several girls she played with who seemed “interesting.” Elsie Blackwood said simply, “It was uneventful.” Wow, hot stuff. Well, it looks like both former Blackwood Boarding girls are off to a great start at their new school!

## Another Routine Schoolyear

Although there has been lots of change with the majority of students at BBSG moving on to other schools, life at the Manor has become routine again. Just like every school year before. Waking up, school, coming home, homework, cleaning, dinner, stalling before bed, and bed. Everyone is missing those long summer days. But thankfully, only 21 more days (but who’s counting?) until the first break of the year, Labor Day! Teacher Flo Blackwood however, is one of the few who has enjoyed getting back into the school year. “I think it’s been really good to get into a routine. It has been a very long time since we’ve been in a routine, so I think it’s been really good." she said “It seems a little lonely, but, it’s pretty nice to just have two people to homeschool. A little less chaotic.” 

## New Giant Fluffy Pillow Chairs

The latest furniture addition at the Manor is probably ranked in the top three favorites for the majority of residents. Two very large bean bags now sit in the basement, perfect to jump on and watch a movie. The new devices have received top ratings and are excellent for relaxation, reading, jumping, or falling asleep. Elsie B. stated, “I was the one who convinced Mom to get them, so you’re welcome people.”
