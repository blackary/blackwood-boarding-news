## Gameday at HHS

The small group of girls who were able to attend the Harrison High School homecoming dance were also able to go to the football game this weekend along with Flo Blackwood. The group cheered (and chatted) from the student section, though most of them couldn’t tell much about the game other than the scoreboard. However, they could tell the Harrison team wasn’t doing too well when three quarters through the game it read an embarrassing scoreline of 35 to…0. Flo stated, “Welllll it was a very sad game. Harrison was struggling.” Even though the game turned out to be a disappointing loss, the crew still had fun cheering them on and hanging out for the evening.

## Blackwood Manor Welcomes Kansas Cityens 

This week Principal Zachary Blackwood’s Aunt and cousin were able to visit the Manor for the first time, and stayed over the weekend. Blackwood Boarding Girls enjoyed crocheting with their Great Aunt and cousin once-removed as usual, as well as catching up with their lives in Kansas City. Everyone also loved eating out at a scrumptious Mexican outdoor restaurant. Overall, it was a fun time to get to see relatives and have quality time chatting and crafting. 

## Schools Out at Blackwood Manor!

BBSG, LCS, and HHS students all trudged slowly through this past week as they made their way to the long-awaited fall break of 2026! Most of the students had already taken most of their tests the week before, and weren’t doing much in class but working on homework that the teachers had given out so they wouldn’t lose their jobs. All except Flo Blackwood of course, who couldn’t be fired either way. But now everyone has made it, and survived! We are all ready for a lovely whole week without any school.
