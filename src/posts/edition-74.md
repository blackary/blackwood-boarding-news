## Magill Cousins Return

This week was the exciting visit of the Blackwood Boarding girls’ Magill (second) cousins, who have now come back to the U.S. for good! A few of you may remember their last visit, back in Edition #22. Unfortunately, this time there was very little eating of ice cream, however it was still a fun time slumber party-ing and of course constantly playing with the new kittens. There was also a bit of swimming and canoeing in Lake Felicity, even though it was a bit chilly out. While they will be moving to California, BBSG students are still excited to have our second cousins in the country, and maybe be able to see them more often. 

## New Home on Wheels for the Chickies

What may have looked like a neat, sturdy chicken house in the back of the Manor with its picturesque coop and chicken swing turned out to be nothing but a rip-off flimsy hut that probably couldn’t hold a flock of chickens for more than six months. Though Flo Blackwood was hesitant, principal Zachary did order an uglier (but sturdier) box-like mobile chicken coop. The new hen house did take a while to put together, but it now sits in the back yard in all its glory, fully portable with a very large run area, in which even student Lottie B. can sit happily inside of. The chickens seem to be enjoying it, and hopefully we will receive some eggs in return any day now!

## Yet Another Furry Little Animal

Student Emory Blackwood was glad to receive her very own kitten earlier this week from Grandma and Grandpa Blackwood (Dad’s grandparents). Although hers is skinnier than the other two, it makes up for its small size in fluff. Emory calls the little orange furball Sage because apparently it “has sage eyes.” However they appear more like grey to Emory’s sisters. “\[Sage] is cute and adorable and nice and fluffy.” said Emory. The trio of kittens seem to be doing well now, happily romping around the garage, and sometimes being strangled by little girls.
