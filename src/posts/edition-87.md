## Stolen Property “Returned”!

This week, after many days without a much-needed school stapler, Principal Z. Blackwood took the liberty of ordering a brand new paper-fastening device on Amazon. When the stapler arrived, he took it up to the office himself to place the new piece of equipment in its new home inside the junk drawer. However, as he entered the office doors, the principal’s eyes fell upon the original stapler, which was thought to be lost forever, right on the floor next to the junk drawer! It appears that all BBSG students and staff may need to have their eyes checked before any similar cases occur…

## School Lawn Transformation

After many months (and years) of digging up dirt and mud and creating a yard of, put simply, utter chaos, the builders and landscapers of Blackwood Boarding have finally been able to begin lawn decor, planting bushes, flowers, grass, and even a small tree! Head custodian and teacher Flo Blackwood has especially (as usual)  been the most excited with their progress, and is loving the new look. “Outstanding job! They are just really transforming dirt to beauty. It’s looking like a tranquil garden.” says Flo. 

## Wallpaper Extraordinaire Begins Official Courier

Flo Blackwood has been sub-contracted for her first wallpapering job outside the school walls. The room she papered is a large bathroom now decorated with gray-blue zebra stripes and leopard print. Now that she has begun her official courier as a wallpaper, Flo plans to continue sub-contracting through A & H Handyman Co., so contact them with any of your wallpapering needs!
