## Going Loco at Hoco

All former BBSG students currently attending Harrison High School were able to have a great time at this year’s homecoming dance along with Katherine Spirydovich and Jane Lutz. Of course the group took plenty of photos beforehand (after the much-needed help of Maja Spirydovich with hair curling) with Elizabeth Spirydovich as the amazing photographer! When all the pictures were finally taken everyone enjoyed going to the dance where they partied for over an hour, before heading back to the Spirydovich household to watch a movie and have some delectable pizza, chocolate, and sparkly grape juice! 

## Autumn Adventures at the Pumpkin Patch 

The BBSG and LCS students had the day off earlier this week and got a free pass from Brain Tree to visit Beezlee's Orchard with the Hart cousins. Taking advantage of the Autumn season, everyone enjoyed riding the tractor to the apple orchard, marigold garden, and pumpkin patch. All four Blackwood Manor residents returned with a small pumpkin, two of which were made into pumpkin puree to create some delicious pies later in the year. 

## Latest Manor Building Edition 

Flo Blackwood was thrilled when the back porch swing was installed this week, “just in time for the cooler weather” and watching the colorful leaves. However, she hasn’t personally spent much time on it since her ideal temperature is in the 80’s. But Flo has still enjoyed taking pictures of Blackwood Boarding Girls on the swing from inside the heated house wearing her turtle-neck sweater.
