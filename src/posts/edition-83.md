## Dreams by Florence

Head BBSG teacher Flo Blackwood coordinated her first wedding this week along with her assistant Esther Ritenour. Blackwood Manor was transformed into a lovely day-spa for the bridesmaids to get ready before the ceremony. Flo B. served an abundance of pastries, beverage options, sandwiches, veggie dips, hummus spreads, and more! “She probably bought enough food to feed the whole wedding.” said Principal Z. At the wedding the two coordinators made sure everything was in order and everyone was in their places. They also served food and arranged cupcakes at the reception accompanied by other very professional-looking family members. “Well the bride’s three words that she hoped for the day were, ‘calm, beautiful, and fun’. I feel like everybody nailed it. Hopefully the bride thought that too.” -Flo B.

## A+ Student

Former Blackwood Boarding Girl Felicity B. received an A+ and a chocolate bar this past Friday for showing Raider Day school spirit in English class. Her teacher told the class she was a firm believer in representing your school and that everyone should wear their orange and blue and Harrison logos every Friday for “Raider Day”. It looks like Felicity has learned the secret to good grades in public school. 

## Another Week of Cleaning

Since moving to Blackwood Manor one of the family favorite weekend activities has become..cleaning! Everyone is always just over the moon to spend Saturday (and any extra time on the weekdays) scrubbing toilets and mopping floors. Resident Elsie Blackwood commented, “Way to sugar-coat it ‘till it’s sour.”
